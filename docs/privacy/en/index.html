Privacy Policy

Stow (어디뒀지) · Effective 1 September 2026

한국어

The short version.

  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • We do not collect advertising IDs, location, or contacts.
  • Your photos and item lists are visible only to members of your household.
  • All data is stored in Seoul, South Korea (AWS ap-northeast-2).

1. What we collect

a. Account

DataEmail address, display name, profile photo URL
SourceProvided by Google when you sign in with Google, or entered by you for email link sign-in
PurposeIdentify you, and show household members who changed what

b. Content you enter

DataNames and notes for your household, places, boxes and items; item photos; quantities; purchase links; categories
PurposeThe core function of the app — remembering where your things are

c. Change history

DataWhich item changed, when, by whom, and how
PurposeHouseholds share one space, so members can see who moved something

d. Device

DataPush notification token
PurposeSending notifications (only if you enable them)

2. What we do not collect

  • Advertising identifiers (AAID) — not collected.
  • Location / GPS — not collected.
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS — not collected.
  • Behavioural analytics SDKs (Google Analytics and similar) — not used.

3. Permissions

CameraTo photograph your items. Only the photo you take is stored.
Photos / media
(Android 12 and below)		To pick an existing photo. Only the photo you select is read.
Internet / network stateTo sync data between household members.
VibrateFor notifications.

Permissions are requested the first time the relevant feature is used. Declining them leaves the rest of the app working.

4. Processors and third parties

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use the following processors:

SupabaseDatabase, authentication and photo storage. Hosted in Seoul, South Korea (AWS ap-northeast-2).
GoogleGoogle account sign-in, only if you choose it.
ResendSending sign-in link emails. Receives your email address only (routed via AWS Tokyo).

5. Retention and deletion

  • Deleted items, boxes and places stay recoverable in Trash for 30 days, then are permanently removed automatically.
  • When you delete your account, if other members remain in your household, ownership transfers to another member and your account data is removed. If nobody remains, the entire household — including all items and photos — is deleted.
  • Deletion is irreversible.

6. Your rights

  • Access and correction — everything you stored is visible and editable in the app.
  • Deletion — delete individual entries, or More → Delete account to remove everything. See Delete your account and data for requesting without the app.
  • Withdrawing consent — deleting your account withdraws consent.

7. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The database enforces row-level security: if you are not a member of a household, the server returns nothing — access is blocked server-side, not just hidden in the app.
  • Photos live in a private bucket and are served through short-lived signed URLs issued only to authorised members.
  • Sign-in uses PKCE, so an intercepted sign-in link cannot be used on another device.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact jkinject@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update and its effective date on this page, and notify you in the app for significant changes.
