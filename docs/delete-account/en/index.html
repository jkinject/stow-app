You can delete your account from inside the app. If you already uninstalled it or cannot sign in, email us instead.

In the app

  1. Open Stow
  2. Tap More (bottom right)
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom
  4. Confirm — it takes effect immediately

What gets deleted

Always deleted

  • Your sign-in account, including your email address. You will not be able to sign in again.
  • Your name and profile photo. Your display name becomes "Former member".

If you were the only member

The entire household is deleted:

  • All places, boxes, items and every photo
  • Change history, shopping list, invite code

If other members remain

Their household must keep working, so the following stays:

Kept Items, places, boxes and photos you added — they belong to the remaining household. In the change history you appear only as "Former member".
Transferred If you were the owner, ownership passes to another member automatically.

Anything that identifies you — email, name, photo — is removed in this case too.

Timing

Account dataImmediately
Household, items, photosImmediately (if you were the only member)
Items already in TrashPermanently removed 30 days after they were deleted
BackupsRemoved from operational backups within 30 days

⚠ Deletion cannot be undone. Signing up again with the same email will not restore your previous data.
