Where did I put that?

Stow remembers which room and which box you put things in. Snap a photo, type a name, and find it in one search — with your whole family.

Get it on Google Play

Free · No ads · No analytics trackers

Not an organizing app

You already have a sensible place for the winter coats, the camping gear, the kids' old toys and the spare cables. The problem is remembering it six months later. Stow is a memory for your house, not a to-do list for tidying it.

How it works

1. Snap and nameTake a photo as you put something away and type its name. Ten seconds.
2. Pick the place and boxPlaces (bedroom, garage, storage unit) hold boxes. Boxes hold things. That's the whole model.
3. Search laterType part of the name. Stow shows the photo and the exact place and box.
4. Scan the boxPrint QR labels, 21 per A4 sheet. Scan one and its contents open instantly.

Built for households

One invite code brings your family into the same home. Everyone sees the same boxes, every change is logged, and you can keep an office or a second home as a separate space. Expiry dates remind you before food or medicine runs out. Deleted things stay in Trash for 30 days.

FAQ

Is Stow free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no analytics trackers. Your photos are stored privately for your household only.

Does it work without internet?

Your last list stays available offline for searching. Adding items needs a connection; photos taken offline upload later automatically.

Can I use it for a storage unit, office or garage?

Yes. A "space" can be anything: your home, an office, a storage unit. Each space has its own places, boxes and members.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Stow is on Android today; iOS is planned.

How do the QR labels work?

Every box gets a QR code. Print them from the app (21 per A4 sheet), cut, stick. Scanning with the phone camera opens that box's contents.
